## Career Goals
My ultimate career goal is to become a legal data scientist and work in a law office or for the US court system. As technology and AI become more intertwined with our courts, I want to make sure that the confidentiality and privacy the public is promised stays intact. Court records and legal files hold some of the most personal information people have, and I believe the people handling that data need to understand both the technology and the responsibility that comes with it.

When I analyzed two data scientist job postings earlier in this course, I noticed that both required SQL, Python or R, statistical analysis, and the ability to explain findings to technical and non-technical audiences. Since those skills show up in almost every data science role, I am focusing on them first. I am building my SQL and Python skills now, and I am starting a beginner project exploring court case data so I can practice those skills on the kind of data I want to work with. I am also planning my own research project on how AI risk assessment tools are used in pretrial and sentencing decisions and whether they affect fairness, comparing rural and urban courts in Oregon.

To get real experience, I am looking for internships in data and business intelligence. I am also deciding on a minor, with criminology, psychology, or cybersecurity management as my top options, since each one would help me understand a different side of this work. After I graduate in spring 2028, I plan to earn a master's degree so I can keep building my knowledge before stepping into the legal field. Since legal data science is still a newer field, I may start as a business intelligence or financial analyst first and then move into legal data work once I have more experience.
