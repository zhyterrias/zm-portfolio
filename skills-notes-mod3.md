What surprised me most was how two roles with the same "Data Scientist" title can focus on such different types of math and business work. Quantitive modeling role is a classic machine learning and product engineering position. It focuses on the technical side, like deep learning, clustering, classification, and causal inference, to improve how the product works and keep users coming back. Business Data Scientist role, on the other hand, is really more of a business operations and finance strategist who happens to know and work within engineering, while the second posting barely mentions machine learning at all. Instead it asks for financial knowledge, market sizing, pricing rate cards, and managing the risk of incentive fund investments.
